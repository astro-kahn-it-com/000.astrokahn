### Chapter 5: Omni Models vs. First-Frame Generation Workflows

In the Omni workflow, the AI is given a comprehensive character sheet and is expected to infer the character's appearance from various angles automatically. While efficient, this can sometimes lead to slight variations or a loss of precise control. Conversely, the manual workflow involves generating distinct turnaround images (front, back, side) and intentionally using the specific angle that matches the desired shot as the direct reference. This means if a shot requires the character to be seen from behind, the rear-facing reference is explicitly used. This painstaking, shot-by-shot methodology prevents the AI from having to guess or interpolate facial features it shouldn't see, resulting in a more controlled, deliberate final sequence.
