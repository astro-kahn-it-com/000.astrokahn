### Chapter 3: Mastering the Re-order Business
A pivotal theme in DeJoria's success story is his profound understanding and implementation of the "re-order business" model. He realized early on that a one-time sale might generate immediate revenue, but true, sustainable wealth is built on creating products that consumers consume and need to purchase repeatedly. This principle was the cornerstone of Paul Mitchell, where salon professionals and their clients constantly needed to replenish their hair care supplies. He applied this exact same logic to his next major venture, the Patrón Spirits Company. By creating an ultra-premium tequila, he wasn't just selling a bottle; he was creating an experience that consumers would want to revisit. The focus shifted from the transaction of selling to the relationship of providing a product that consistently delivers quality, thereby ensuring continuous re-orders. This strategic focus is a critical takeaway for any entrepreneur looking to build long-term value.
