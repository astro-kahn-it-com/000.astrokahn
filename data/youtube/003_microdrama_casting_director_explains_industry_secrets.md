In this insightful episode of the Verticulture Podcast, the hosts delve deep into the fast-paced and rapidly evolving world of microdramas. By dissecting the nuances of casting for bite-sized, highly engaging content, the episode provides a comprehensive look at what it takes to succeed in the microdrama ecosystem, from auditioning to final production.
